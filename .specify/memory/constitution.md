# Project Constitution: Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

## Title
Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

## Purpose
Generate a complete high-level technical book + full Docusaurus website on “Physical AI”—AI systems that operate in real physical environments using ROS 2, Gazebo, Unity, NVIDIA Isaac, and Vision-Language-Action (VLA).

## Goals
- Teach embodied intelligence and Physical AI fundamentals
- Train students to design, simulate, and deploy humanoid robots
- Integrate AI models (LLMs + VLA) with robotics systems
- Build a complete capstone: An autonomous humanoid robot

## Audience
Beginner–intermediate robotics and AI students, universities, bootcamps, and devs transitioning from digital AI to embodied robotics.

## Tone
- Technical, clear, instructional
- Real engineering examples
- ROS 2 code, Gazebo configs, Isaac scripts, VLA pipelines
- Clean Markdown and Docusaurus-ready formatting

## Major Book Parts
Part I: Physical AI Foundations
Part II: Module 1 — ROS 2: The Robotic Nervous System
Part III: Module 2 — Digital Twin with Gazebo & Unity
