# Preface

## Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics: A Comprehensive Guide

Welcome to "Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics," a comprehensive guide designed to take you from foundational concepts to advanced implementations in the rapidly evolving field of intelligent humanoid robotics.

### About This Book

This book serves as both an educational resource and practical guide for developing intelligent humanoid robotic systems. It combines theoretical foundations with hands-on implementation, covering the complete development lifecycle from basic ROS 2 concepts to advanced Vision-Language-Action systems.

### Target Audience

This book is designed for:
- Robotics engineers and researchers
- AI and machine learning practitioners
- Computer science students and educators
- Hardware engineers working on robotic platforms
- Anyone interested in the intersection of AI and physical systems

### Book Structure

The content is organized into progressive modules:

1. **Foundations**: Core concepts of Physical AI and the integration of AI with robotics
2. **ROS 2 Development**: Practical ROS 2 concepts including nodes, topics, services, URDF modeling, and advanced workflows
3. **Digital Twin**: Simulation environments using Gazebo for testing and development
4. **Isaac AI Platform**: NVIDIA's Isaac Sim for advanced robotics simulation
5. **Vision-Language-Action Systems**: Integration of perception, reasoning, and action
6. **Hardware Specifications**: Requirements and recommendations for development workstations
7. **Capstone Project**: A comprehensive project integrating all concepts

### How to Use This Book

We recommend following the modules sequentially for beginners, as each builds upon the previous. Advanced practitioners may skip to specific modules based on their needs. Each module includes practical examples and exercises to reinforce learning.

### Prerequisites

Readers should have:
- Basic programming experience (preferably Python)
- Understanding of fundamental mathematics (linear algebra, calculus)
- Basic knowledge of robotics concepts (helpful but not required)

### Technical Requirements

- Modern computer with sufficient processing power for simulation
- Ubuntu 20.04/22.04 or equivalent Linux environment
- Access to development tools covered in the hardware section
